CAIRN Dual + HamView — SenseCAP Indicator distribution

This is an independent CAIRN-inspired UI implementation. It is not the original
CAIRN firmware and does not claim complete CAIRN feature parity. The repository
was originally populated with CAIRN documentation; those inherited documents
remain reference material for the upstream project, not this build's support list.
